The Case of Natalie’s Missing Activity Kit

Story created by Kerlyn, Kristen and Leia

Two families went to McDonald’s for supper. While the adults waited for their orders, Natalie and two other children played in the playground. At 7.12 p.m., Natalie’s activity kit disappeared from the bench beside the play area.

Staff later found the kit intact at the cashier’s counter, where lost items are kept. Find out who moved it, which item identified that person and where they picked it up.

Suspects

  • Sally was working on a school project while waiting for her order.
  • Oliver was helping clear tables.
  • Alex was helping set up decorations for a birthday party.
  • Chloe was the manager on duty.

Objects & Locations

Objects

  • Black costume mask
  • Paper apple
  • Craft cord
  • Cooking pot

Locations

  • Dine-in table
  • Kitchen
  • Playground
  • Washroom entrance

Evidence

At 7.12 p.m., the four suspects were in different locations and each was holding a different item from the list. Exactly one of them moved the kit.

  1. A family photo taken at 7.12 p.m. shows someone lifting the kit off the playground bench. A play panel hides the person’s face, but a paper apple with a black seed drawn on it is visible in their other hand.
  2. At 7.12 p.m., Sally was at the dine-in table holding a black costume mask for her school project. She later left it at the cashier’s counter when she picked up her order.
  3. An order photo shows Chloe holding a cooking pot in the kitchen at 7.12 p.m.
  4. At 7.12 p.m., Alex was holding a roll of craft cord by the washroom entrance while a colleague brought over the party decorations.

Teacher Solution

Oliver moved the activity kit from the playground. He was holding the paper apple.

At 7.12 p.m., Sally had the mask at the dine-in table, Chloe had the pot in the kitchen and Alex had the craft cord by the washroom entrance. That leaves Oliver in the playground with the paper apple. The photo shows that person picking up the kit.

Oliver thought the kit had been left behind and took it to the cashier’s counter. Natalie collected it there.
